## Bottom line up front
Macao's political risk is Low and stable over the next 12 months. Beijing's control is firm, the next Chief Executive election is not due until 2029, and the fiscal reserve held MOP666.74 billion at the end of 2025. The main driver is casino revenue: gross gaming revenue (GGR) fell year on year for the fifth month running in October 2026, and gaming taxes supply 84% of government revenue. I would move the outlook to Deteriorating if GGR fell more than 10% year on year for three consecutive months, or if US-China tensions reached the three US-owned casino concessionaires.

## Key judgments
1. Sam Hou Fai is **almost certain** (~97%) to still be Chief Executive on 1 November 2027. He was elected unopposed in October 2024 to a five-year term, and Beijing praised his government's national security record in March 2026.
2. Au Kam San is **very likely** (~85%) to be convicted of at least one national security offence before November 2027. His closed-door trial opened on 16 September 2026, and on 18 September the court said it had concluded and would deliver the verdict at a later date. I found no report of a verdict by early November.
3. The authorities are **unlikely** (~25%) to charge a second person under the national security law before November 2027. Au was the first person arrested under the law as widened in 2023, but his charges include ties with foreign groups and media, which leaves residents with similar contacts exposed.
4. GGR for January to October 2027 is **likely** (~60%) to be lower than the MOP208.80 billion of the same months of 2026. Seaport puts recent softness partly down to China's weaker economy and tax enforcement affecting offshore trusts, CICC saw no clear near-term inflection point in October, and early 2027 will be measured against months of growth in early 2026.
5. DSEC's first estimate of 2026 real GDP growth is **very likely** (~85%) to come in below the IMF's July projection of 3.3%. Growth slowed to 0.3% in the second quarter and 0.1% in the third, leaving 2.9% for the first nine months, so reaching 3.3% for the year would need fourth-quarter growth of more than 4%.

## Drivers
### Political
Sam, a former top judge and the first Chief Executive born in mainland China, won 394 of the 398 votes cast in the Election Committee in 2024. The September 2025 Legislative Assembly election was the first under 2024 amendments that give national security officials the final say on candidates: 12 hopefuls were barred and turnout was 53%. In October 2025 Sam made Wong Sio Chak Secretary for Administration and Justice and appointed André Cheong, Wong's predecessor in the post, to the legislature, which elected him president with all 33 votes cast, the first time an appointed newcomer had been chosen.

In March 2026 Xia Baolong, who oversees Hong Kong and Macao affairs for Beijing, urged Sam's government to draw up Macao's third five-year plan in full compliance with national strategy; the plan, published on 18 August, aims to raise non-gaming industries to about 60% of GDP by 2030.

On 19 March 2026 the legislature unanimously passed a law that lets judges hear national security cases in camera and requires defence lawyers to obtain permission from national security officials. Au, 69, a former legislator and organiser of the city's Tiananmen vigils, was detained in July 2025 and charged with subversion, which carries up to 25 years. On 23 and 24 September the government and the legislature rejected the EU's 2025 annual report on Macao over its comments on the 2025 election, and urged the EU to stop issuing reports that interfere in China's internal affairs.

### Security
Violence is negligible: the model shows no battle deaths in 2024 and 0.56 homicides per 100,000 in 2023. The risk for foreigners is legal exposure under the national security framework.

### Economic
GGR rose 9.1% to MOP247.4 billion in 2025. October 2026 brought MOP21.68 billion, down 10.0% despite the Golden Week holiday, though ten-month GGR was still up 1.6%. Third-quarter GGR fell 3.8%, with mass-market baccarat down 7.9%. The 2026 forecast of MOP236 billion is safe, as November and December need only MOP27.2 billion between them, but a weak 2027 could force a midyear cut like June 2025's, from MOP240 billion to MOP228 billion.

Gaming taxes reached MOP74.56 billion in the first nine months of 2026, 84.1% of government revenue. Of 32.2 million visitors in that period, 23.9 million came from the mainland, so mainland policy on travel, payments or offshore income moves revenue faster than anything decided in Macao. Fitch wrote in April 2025 that a forced sale of US operators' Macao businesses could become more plausible if US-China relations worsened, though it saw no sign of China targeting them.

The six concessions run to the end of 2032; the last satellite casino closed on 1 January 2026. In March the regulator said it would fine-tune the scope of the concessionaires' MOP108.7 billion non-gaming pledges. The Hengqin cooperation zone had more than 8,000 Macao-funded firms by June 2026 and more than 30,000 Macao residents living, working or studying there in February, but it does not yet offset a casino downturn.

### Social
The model puts unemployment at 2.35% and youth unemployment at 6.78% in 2025. Open dissent is rare, though the 2025 election produced record numbers of blank (5,987) and invalid (7,077) ballots.

## Indicators to watch
- The 2027 Policy Address on 16 November, the GGR forecast in the 2027 budget, and any midyear revision.
- Monthly GGR down more than 10% year on year, or a fall in mainland arrivals.
- The verdict in Au Kam San's case, and any new national security arrest.
- Mainland measures on offshore income, cross-border payments or travel permits.
- US measures naming Macao officials, or Chinese measures naming US casino companies.
- DSEC's full-year 2026 GDP estimate.

## Why my rating differs from the model
My rating matches the model's tier: a composite of 14 (Low), 200th of 203 countries, with no red flags and no watchlist events. The score rose 0.6 points on last year. Since 2015 it has entered Moderate only in 2020 (20) and 2022 (21), both years when COVID-19 hit casino revenue, so the shocks that move Macao's score come from outside the city.

The economic pillar (11) rests on IMF figures for 2026: growth of 3.3% (too high, on judgment 5), a fiscal surplus of 8.4% of GDP and a current-account surplus of 39.8%. Governance (27) scores worst of the four pillars, with voice and accountability at about 50 in 2025, data that predate the March 2026 law and the September trial. The model misses concentration: one industry and one source market fund the government. With thin buffers that would justify Moderate; with the reserve and no government debt, I keep Low.

## Implications
For casino operators and lenders the risk is policy: tax and investment obligations are set in Macao, demand by mainland policy. US-owned operators should plan for US-China tensions reaching their concessions before 2032. Foreign governments, NGOs and journalists should assume that contacts with Macao residents can feature in national security cases, and that defence lawyers in those cases need permission from national security officials.
