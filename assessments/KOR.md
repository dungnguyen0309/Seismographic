## Bottom line up front
South Korea's political risk is Low and stable over the next 12 months. Yoon Suk Yeol's insurrection appeal is in its final hearings, and the main domestic risk is the ruling Democratic Party's (DP) fight with prosecutors and the Supreme Court, including a bill, due for a vote on 5 November, to set up a special counsel on the indictments against President Lee Jae-myung. I would move to Moderate if the chief justice were impeached, if Lee's cases were dropped, or if North Korean fire or mines killed South Koreans.

## Key judgments
1. Yoon Suk Yeol's insurrection conviction is **likely** (~75%) to be made final by the Supreme Court before November 2027. He received a life sentence in February, the special counsel wants the death penalty on appeal, and the Seoul High Court will set a sentencing date after his final statement on 5 November.
2. Prosecutors or a special counsel are **unlikely** (~25%) to withdraw any indictment against Lee before November 2027. The DP can pass its bill alone, but an April draft that let the counsel cancel indictments drew a public backlash, and in September Lee asked the Assembly not to seek cancellation of his cases.
3. The National Assembly is **very unlikely** (~15%) to impeach Chief Justice Cho Hee-dae before November 2027. On 27 October the DP chose a parliamentary probe of Cho instead, amid concerns about a backlash from moderate voters.
4. Donald Trump and Kim Jong-un are **unlikely** (~25%) to meet before November 2027. Trump said in August he would meet Kim later this year, but Kim Yo-jong called his cut to the summer drills "unworthy of comment", and on 31 October the FBI linked the North to the theft of about $6.3 billion from Binance two days earlier. I hold this with low confidence.
5. North Korean fire or mines are **very unlikely** (~10%) to kill a South Korean before November 2027. The three soldiers injured in September were hurt by mines laid during the North's border works, and Seoul answered by pressing for dialogue.
6. There is a **roughly even chance** (~50%) that exports in at least one month before November 2027 come in below the same month a year earlier. October exports were 93.7% up on a year before on chip sales, a high base for 2027, and the KOSPI fell 3.59% on 3 November on doubts about AI spending.

## Drivers
### Political
The DP won 12 of 16 metropolitan mayor and governor posts in the 3 June local elections, up from 5 of 17 in 2022. The People Power Party (PPP) held Seoul, where Oh Se-hoon won a fifth term by about one point. Ballot papers ran out at polling stations around the country, and a special counsel is examining 2.47 million ballots. The next national vote is the Assembly election, provisionally on 12 April 2028.

Lee's approval hovered around 60% at the time of the June vote. Gallup Korea put it at a record low of 37% in late September and early October and at 40% on 23 October, with real estate policy, the economy and security the main complaints. The DP's special counsel would examine whether prosecutors under Yoon fabricated evidence against Lee, whose trials were suspended when he took office, and PPP floor leader Jeong Jeom-sig has threatened to filibuster every bill tabled on 5 November. The DP accuses Cho of meddling in the June 2025 presidential election, after his court overturned Lee's acquittal in an election law case shortly before the vote.

### Security
On 21 September two mines exploded about 10 metres south of the Military Demarcation Line in the western DMZ, injuring three South Korean soldiers, and the military and the UN Command later concluded that North Korea had planted them deliberately. In October the North said it would resume propaganda broadcasts and deploy more strike capabilities along the border, then tested a cluster bomb on 31 October and a dispersal warhead for a cruise missile on 2 November.

On 17 August Trump told the Pentagon to scale back joint exercises, saying Seoul had declined to help with the US war on Iran, and the allies cut Ulchi Freedom Shield to half its planned length. After US visas for two DP lawmakers were held up in October, Han Dong-hoon, an independent conservative, said relations with the US were "in a state of grave crisis".

### Economic
Exports reached a record $115.3 billion in October, nearly half of it chips. GDP grew 3.4% year on year in the third quarter, and the IMF forecasts 3.3% for 2026 and 2.8% for 2027. The 2027 budget of 820.9 trillion won, 12.8% more than this year, assumes revenue rises 30.4%, partly on chip-driven tax receipts. In mid-September the $350 billion US investment pledge still had no finalised project.

### Social
Real gross domestic income rose 18.8% year on year in the third quarter while private consumption shrank 0.2% in the quarter. Lee presents the budget, which raises youth spending 53.5%, as a remedy for "K-shaped growth".

## Indicators to watch
- The Seoul High Court's sentence for Yoon, and the speed of the Supreme Court appeal.
- Any request to withdraw an indictment against Lee.
- An impeachment motion against Cho Hee-dae reaching a plenary vote.
- Findings of the ballot special counsel, whose 150-day mandate runs into early 2027.
- The first month in which exports fall year on year.
- New mine blasts or shooting at the DMZ.
- US decisions on next year's joint exercises.

## Implications
Investors in Korean chipmakers should plan for a turn in memory prices, which would also cut some of the tax revenue behind the 2027 budget. Firms bidding for projects under the US investment pledge should expect the US president to choose them and funds to move no sooner than 45 business days after Washington notifies Seoul. Governments should expect joint drills to turn more on Washington's decisions.
