## Bottom line up front
I rate Azerbaijan's political risk Elevated and stable over the next 12 months, the same tier as the model (47, 76th of 203 countries). Ilham Aliyev faces no challenge at home, oil prices far above the budget's $65 assumption keep the state flush, and the border with Armenia has had no serious incident in a long time. The risks are a possible return to war in Iran, a relationship with Russia that keeps breaking down, and a crackdown that has jailed at least 25 journalists and now holds the leader of the opposition Popular Front in an alleged coup case. A signed peace treaty with Armenia would move me towards Moderate; a second strike from Iranian territory or a trade rupture with Moscow would move me towards High.

## Key judgments
1. Azerbaijan and Armenia sign the peace agreement initialled in Washington in August 2025 before 12 November 2027: **very unlikely** (~15%). Baku will not sign until Armenia removes from its constitution what Baku sees as an implicit territorial claim. A new Armenian constitution is expected by the end of 2026, but it needs a two-thirds vote in parliament and then a referendum, and the Caspian Policy Center sees no clear path to success.
2. Someone is killed in fighting on the Armenia-Azerbaijan border before 12 November 2027: **very unlikely** (~7%). Both governments now gain from the peace: 105,000 tonnes of cargo has crossed Azerbaijan to Armenia since November 2025, and a joint commission is delimiting the border. The figure stays above 5% because the treaty is unsigned and the border maps are not yet agreed.
3. Ali Karimli, chairman of the Azerbaijan Popular Front Party, is convicted and given a prison term before 12 November 2027: **likely** (~70%). The Toplum TV case and the case against Ramiz Mehdiyev, in which Karimli is also charged, both ended in conviction this year. The doubt is speed: Meclis.info's founder was arrested in April 2024 and prosecutors only sought a sentence in October 2026.
4. Relations with Russia break openly again before 12 November 2027, meaning Baku recalls its ambassador, Moscow restricts Azerbaijani food imports, or either side detains a group of the other's citizens in a new case: **unlikely** (~25%). An April joint statement settled the AZAL crash, compensation included, yet in August Baku put three Russian television commentators on a wanted list and summoned the Russian ambassador. Neither side gains from a full break; trade was about $5 billion in 2025.
5. A drone or missile launched from Iranian territory hits Azerbaijan again before 12 November 2027: **very unlikely** (~15%). A call between the presidents defused the 5 March strike on Nakhchivan, and Baku says it will not join a war on Iran. But the US blockade now reaches Iran's land borders and the Caspian.
6. The State Oil Fund (SOFAZ) reports assets of $70 billion or more in its last figures before 12 November 2027: **very likely** (~85%). Assets are about $74 billion, the 2027 budget cuts the transfer from the fund and again assumes $65 oil, and Azerbaijani crude sold for $120 to $130 in October.

## Drivers
### Political
Aliyev's position is secure. ODIHR found that the 2024 early presidential and parliamentary elections took place without genuine political alternatives, and no national vote falls within the horizon. On 22 October a closed court gave Ramiz Mehdiyev, 88, who ran the presidential administration for 24 years, a suspended 14-year sentence for treason, attempting to seize power by force, money laundering and bribery. The verdict, made public on 27 October, refers to an unnamed "foreign state". Karimli and Mammad Ibrahim of the Popular Front are held in the same case in separate proceedings; a preliminary hearing was set for 3 November.

On 27 July a Baku court sentenced nine people in the Toplum TV case to between 12 and 15 years. The Supreme Court upheld the Abzas Media sentences in April, 12 Meydan TV journalists are on trial, and on 20 October prosecutors asked for 12 years for Meclis.info's founder. These cases began with currency smuggling charges, and in the Toplum TV case other financial crimes were added. CPJ counted at least 25 journalists in jail in October. EU talks on a new agreement resumed in April, and both sides say about 90% of the text is agreed.

### Security
The peace with Armenia holds without a treaty. Two-way trade has started, the border commission is working towards agreed maps, and on 12 November in Dublin Aliyev and Armenian Prime Minister Nikol Pashinyan discussed connecting their electricity grids.

The route Washington calls TRIPP and Baku calls the Zangezur corridor is slow. Armenia's parliament ratified the framework agreement 63 to 32 in late October. The US will hold 74% of the development company for an initial 49 years, but its charter is not yet negotiated, and groundbreaking is expected in early 2027.

Iran is the larger danger. US Central Command set up Task Force Iron Gate on 7 November to extend the blockade of Iran's ports to its land borders and the Caspian, and Al Jazeera reports that the threat of renewed war is growing. Azerbaijan's border with Iran is about 700 km long.

### Economic
Growth is weak because oil output is falling. GDP rose 1.5% in January to September 2026, with the oil and gas sector down 1.2% and the rest of the economy up 2.6%. The Azeri-Chirag-Gunashli fields averaged about 320,000 barrels a day, 2.25% less than a year earlier. Prices more than make up for it: Azerbaijani crude averaged $94.5 in the first half and peaked at $133 on 9 October, though Brent fell towards $90 in the second week of November on hopes of a US deal with Iran. Currency and gold losses of 1.87 billion manats held SOFAZ's nine-month gain to $462 million. The 2027 budget plans spending of 42.4 billion manats and a deficit of 3.17 billion. The IMF put central bank and SOFAZ reserves at $85 billion at the end of 2025.

### Social
The return of displaced people to Karabakh and what Baku calls East Zangezur is behind schedule: 40,520 had returned by 1 October, against about 140,000 planned by the end of 2026. Mines are one reason: 979 were found in October alone. From 2027 the income tax rate on the first 2,500 manats of monthly pay in the private non-oil sector rises from 3% to 5%. Youth unemployment was 13.09% in 2025.

## Indicators to watch
- Armenia's draft constitution, due by the end of 2026, and whether its parliament sends it to a referendum.
- The TRIPP company charter and a groundbreaking date.
- US-Iran talks, Iron Gate activity in the Caspian and any incident on the Iranian border.
- The verdicts in the Karimli and Meydan TV cases.
- New detentions of Russian citizens in Baku, or of Azerbaijani community figures in Russia.
- SOFAZ's quarterly assets, and Brent below $65.

## Why my rating differs from the model
My rating matches the model's tier: a composite of 47 (Elevated), 76th of 203 countries, with full data coverage and a fall of 1.4 points on last year. In the app's history, which starts in 2015, the score was 55 in 2015, peaked at 64 (High) in 2020, the year of the 44-day war, and was lowest at 45 in 2024.

The stability pillar (35) leans on WGI political stability, which rose from 55.97 in 2022 to 63.97 in 2025, and on zero battle deaths in 2024. Neither captures the March drone strike on Nakhchivan or the August row with Moscow, and the app's watchlist holds no Azerbaijan events. Governance (64), the second-highest pillar after social, fits: voice and accountability of 29.73 and control of corruption of 32.35 in 2025 predate the Toplum TV verdicts and the Mehdiyev trial. The economic pillar (35) uses 2026 IMF figures that look right: growth of 1.7%, against 1.5% in the first nine months, debt of 16.5% of GDP, a fiscal surplus of 3.7% and a current account surplus of 11.3%. The social pillar (69) is pushed up by the displacement red flag, 7.75% of the population in 2025. I read that as the legacy of the war of the early 1990s rather than new flight, so it overstates current social risk. The two errors roughly cancel out, so I keep Elevated and Stable.

## Implications
Donors should assume that any grant to an Azerbaijani outlet or NGO can be recast as currency smuggling, with long sentences for the recipients. European gas buyers can count on supply: Azerbaijan exported 25.2 bcm in 2025, more than half of it to the EU. Investors in TRIPP should not expect construction before the company's charter is signed, and should price in Iran.
