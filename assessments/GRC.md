## Bottom line up front
I rate Greece's political risk Moderate and stable, one tier below the model's Elevated (40, 121st of 203 countries). The main event is the general election, which Prime Minister Kyriakos Mitsotakis is expected to call for May or June 2027. New Democracy (ND) leads recent polls by about 14 points but stands near 30%, short of a majority, so a repeat vote is likely and a caretaker government could be in office when Greece takes the EU Council presidency on 1 July 2027. Energy-driven inflation and Turkish objections to the Crete-Cyprus cable surveys add pressure. A parliament with no majority after two votes, or Turkish ships stopping the surveys, would move me to Elevated.

## Key judgments
1. ND wins 151 or more of the 300 seats at the first general election of 2027: **unlikely** (~30%). Opinion Poll's October projection gave ND 122 seats on 30.3%, Metron Analysis and Pulse both had it at 30% in polls published on 22 October, and MARC had it at 30.5% on 12 November. Mitsotakis argues against coalition governments and rules out working with parties to ND's right, and PASOK's congress ruled out a coalition with ND, so a shortfall means a second vote.
2. Greece holds at least two general elections before 1 November 2027: **likely** (~70%). This is the reverse of judgment 1, because no likely partner would give ND a coalition after a first-round shortfall. ND needed a second vote in June 2023, and Alexis Tsipras's camp treats one as close to a given.
3. Mitsotakis is prime minister, other than as caretaker, on 1 November 2027: **likely** (~70%). In Opinion Poll's October survey 27.4% preferred him as prime minister and 13.1% preferred Tsipras. The risk is a second inconclusive vote that leaves a coalition without him.
4. The seabed surveys for the Crete-Cyprus electricity cable are suspended because of Turkish pressure before Greece's navigation notice expires on 6 March 2027: **unlikely** (~25%). Surveys stopped in 2024 with about 60% done. Ankara has issued a counter-notice, and on 12 November the frigate TCG Gökova, which had been shadowing the survey ship, read a statement over the radio, but Athens does not expect physical obstruction.
5. S&P, Moody's or Fitch lowers Greece's rating or moves its outlook to negative before 1 November 2027: **very unlikely** (~10%). All three rate Greece investment grade, Moody's with a positive outlook, and the 2027 draft budget keeps a primary surplus. It would take sustained loosening after the election or months without a government.

## Drivers
### Political
ND has governed alone since June 2023, and the opposition remains split. In Opinion Poll's survey of 7-12 October, Tsipras's Greek Left Alliance (ELAS) had 15.9% and PASOK 12.3%, with nine parties projected to enter parliament. Maria Karystianou's Hope for Democracy had a 10.4% projection in Metron Analysis's June poll; Opinion Poll put it at 4.5% in October. Mitsotakis says he would take responsibility if ND fell below 25%.

Two cases keep ND on the defensive. In the OPEKEPE farm subsidy affair, parliament lifted the immunity of 11 MPs in April, and in July the European Public Prosecutor's Office charged 22 people, among them four sitting MPs. On the 2023 Tempi rail crash, the trial of 36 defendants in Larissa, open since 23 March, was hearing witnesses in early November. On 30 October parliament drew the judges of a special court that will try former deputy minister Christos Triantopoulos and others over the alleged alteration of evidence at the crash site, possibly starting before the end of December. The crash anniversary in February brought rallies in more than 70 cities.

### Security
Mitsotakis and Recep Tayyip Erdoğan met in Ankara in February, where officials signed cooperation agreements, with no movement on the 1995 casus belli or maritime boundaries. On 20 October Libya's eastern House of Representatives approved the 2019 Türkiye-Libya maritime memorandum. Greece, the EU, Cyprus, Egypt, Bahrain and the UAE called it invalid at the UN, and Türkiye defends it. The cable survey ship Abeille Marengo left Heraklion on 10 November. Türkiye says part of its survey area lies on Turkish continental shelf, and Athens says no Turkish permission is needed. On 12 November, as the ship entered waters south-east of Karpathos, the Turkish frigate shadowing it restated that claim by radio.

Irregular arrivals fell to 41,998 in the year to July 2026, from 61,123 a year earlier, by migration ministry figures; the Libya-Crete route is now the main pressure point.

### Economic
The October draft budget forecasts growth of 2.0% in 2026 and 2.3% in 2027, a primary surplus of 3.3% and an overall surplus of 0.3% of GDP in 2027, and debt falling from 136.8% to 128.8% of GDP. Energy prices have risen sharply since the US and Israel went to war with Iran on 28 February. Consumer inflation reached 5.7% in October, with natural gas up 80% on a year earlier. On 30 September Mitsotakis asked Brussels to let governments exclude temporary support from EU spending limits.

### Social
In MARC's June poll, 73.8% named inflation and the cost of living among their main concerns, against 31.4% for corruption and 13.6% for illegal migration. Unemployment was 8.5% in 2025, and 21.4% among 15 to 24-year-olds.

## Indicators to watch
- The election date, and whether Mitsotakis keeps to May or moves to June.
- ND's projected seats in the main polls, from 122 in October.
- Whether the Turkish frigate shadowing the Abeille Marengo goes beyond radio statements, or Greece pauses the surveys.
- Monthly consumer inflation from ELSTAT, at 5.7% in October.
- The start of the Triantopoulos special court and new EPPO steps in the OPEKEPE case.

## Why my rating differs from the model
The model scores Greece 40 (Elevated), 121st of 203, up 0.6 points on last year and just above the line between Moderate and Elevated. Since the app's history begins in 2015 the score has stayed between 38.2 and 42.5 and has switched tier every year since 2020, so I read Greece as a border case.

The economic pillar (66) carries the score through three red flags. Reserves of 2.0 months of imports (2025) mean little for a euro area member that imports and borrows in euros, and the 6.6% current account deficit (2026) is financed in the same currency. Debt of 136.0% of GDP (2026) is high, but six agencies rate Greece investment grade and the debt ratio is falling. The stability (22) and governance (34) pillars are low.

The model also misses some risk. Its 2026 average inflation figure of 4.2% is below October's annual rate of 5.7%, and the app's watchlist holds no Greek events, so the election arithmetic, the Türkiye-Libya memorandum and the cable dispute do not register. These keep me at the upper end of Moderate.

## Implications
Firms should expect few new policy decisions from April to the formation of a government, and possibly a caretaker cabinet in July. Energy-intensive businesses should plan for high prices into 2027, and bond investors should watch for a looser post-election budget. Shipping and energy firms operating between Crete, Karpathos and Cyprus should expect more competing navigation notices.
