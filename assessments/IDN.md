## Bottom line up front
Indonesia's political risk is Elevated and stable over the next 12 months, one tier above the model. The Jakarta Composite Index (JCI) lost 41.5% between January and 30 October after MSCI proposed moving Indonesia out of its emerging-market category, the rupiah's reference rate hit a record low on 28 October, and President Prabowo Subianto's approval fell to 44.5%. On 3 November police named his chief of staff a suspect in the alleged tapping of his phone. The main driver is MSCI's decision, due by 30 June 2027. A reclassification with the rupiah past 20,000 per dollar, or a move to lift the 3% deficit ceiling, would take me to High.

## Key judgments
1. There is a **roughly even chance** (~50%) that MSCI announces by 30 June 2027 that it will reclassify Indonesia as a standalone market. The Financial Services Authority (OJK) says MSCI still sees market dysfunction and weak enforcement despite new disclosure and free-float rules, and MSCI's consultation runs to 30 April 2027.
2. The rupiah is **unlikely** (~30%) to pass 20,000 per dollar on Bank Indonesia's JISDOR reference rate before November 2027, from a record 18,183 on 28 October. Reserves were $146.3 billion (5.3 months of imports) in September and foreign money kept flowing into bonds, but Prabowo's brother Hashim Djojohadikusumo has said an MSCI downgrade could mean 20,000 to 21,000.
3. Parliament is **unlikely** (~20%) to relax the 3% deficit ceiling in the State Finance Law before November 2027. The finance ministry defends the cap and the 2027 budget sets a deficit of 2.4% of GDP, though the 2026 deficit is now projected at 2.85% and interest payments equalled about 90% of new borrowing from January to September.
4. Suahasil Nazara is **likely** (~60%) to still be finance minister on 1 November 2027. I hold this with low confidence: he is the third of Prabowo's term, after Sri Mulyani Indrawati (replaced in September 2025) and Purbaya Yudhi Sadewa (replaced in September 2026).
5. Protest violence is **unlikely** (~25%) to kill five or more people in any single month before November 2027, as it did in late August 2025. Protests in 2026 have been frequent but smaller. The most likely trigger is a rise in subsidised fuel prices; the government has raised fuel subsidy spending this year to keep energy costs off households.
6. The last count of people displaced by the Papua conflict published before November 2027 is **likely** (~70%) to be above 100,000. Estimates from July to October 2026 ranged from about 122,000 to 127,031, and troops moved into three districts of Tolikara in October.

## Drivers
### Political
Prabowo, sworn in on 20 October 2024, has replaced his finance minister twice. Suahasil took over from Purbaya on 14 September, and on 26 August the House finance commission approved Destry Damayanti, Prabowo's only nominee, as Bank Indonesia governor for 2026 to 2031 in place of Perry Warjiyo. A reshuffle on 1 October moved Listyo Sigit Prabowo from national police chief to chief of presidential staff.

On 3 November police investigators named Listyo and two senior officers as suspects in the alleged tapping, in 2025 and 2026, of phones belonging to Prabowo, officials, politicians, journalists and NGO figures, and in deleting logs to hide it. All three are detained. Listyo left the police only a month ago, and his arrest suggests a rift inside the security establishment.

Prabowo's approval fell from 81.2% in November 2025 to 44.5% in October, according to the pollster SMRC. In late August 2025 protests over lawmakers' housing allowances spread after a police van ran over a delivery driver; six people died and troops were deployed in Jakarta. The election commission expects the stages of the 2029 election to begin around June 2027, and as of September the election bill was stalled in the House; in January the House shelved a coalition plan to have local councils choose regional heads.

### Security
The conflict in Papua escalated in 2026. Human Rights Monitor recorded 42 attacks and armed clashes in the third quarter, up from 36 in the second, and a Regional Representatives Council committee counted 59 people killed in 2026 by mid-October, 43 of them civilians. Members of the West Papua National Liberation Army (TPNPB) killed US pilot Nicholas Goselin in Yahukimo on 2 July. Troops entered three districts of Tolikara on 5 October with no public explanation of their legal basis, and rights defenders estimate that 8,251 civilians are trapped there.

Outside Papua, the March 2025 amendment to the armed forces law raised the number of civilian institutions open to serving officers from 10 to 14. On 15 October the Constitutional Court pushed the other way, setting an October 2028 deadline to amend the military justice law so that soldiers accused of ordinary crimes face civilian courts.

### Economic
The JCI fell 11.57% on 16 October, despite a 30-minute trading halt, after MSCI opened its consultation on standalone status, its category for markets whose accessibility has severely deteriorated. Foreign investors had sold a net Rp97.92 trillion of stocks in 2026 by 30 October, and the index fell another 3.78% to 4,904 on 3 November.

The rupiah first passed 18,000 per dollar on 4 June, after the US-Israeli war on Iran pushed up oil prices. Indonesia is a net oil importer, and its oil and gas imports were up 37% from January to September. Bank Indonesia raised the BI-Rate from 4.75% to 5.75% between 20 May and 18 June and has held it since.

The 2026 deficit is now projected at 2.85% of GDP against a target of 2.68%, because of fire relief and higher fuel subsidies, and on 3 November the Financial System Stability Committee cut its 2026 growth forecast to 5.4% to 5.5%. The free meals programme, Prabowo's flagship, is shrinking: its 2026 budget fell from Rp335 trillion to Rp229 trillion in two cuts, and in a review that followed a series of mass food poisonings the National Nutrition Agency suspended thousands of kitchens. Danantara, the sovereign fund launched in February 2025 to hold the state's company stakes, now receives the dividends that used to count as budget revenue. Energy minister Bahlil Lahadalia has put electricity exports on hold, stalling the power sales to Singapore that Danantara oversees.

### Social
The model's labour data look benign (unemployment of 3.24% and youth unemployment of 12.98% in 2025), but volatile food inflation reached 5.81% in October. Fires have burned about 1.9 million hectares this year, and the area burned from January to September was about 38% larger than in the same period of 2019. CREA estimates that fire smoke caused about 3,560 deaths in September. Haze, a cut in gas exports and Bahlil's call to stop routing exports through third countries have strained relations with Singapore. On 20 October, the second anniversary of Prabowo's inauguration, students protested in Jakarta, Bandung and Yogyakarta over the fires, the cost of living and the market rout; 16 police officers were hurt in Bandung, and police detained 19 people at a Jakarta protest on 28 October.

## Indicators to watch
- MSCI's review on 11 and 12 November and its decision by 30 June 2027.
- The JISDOR rate passes 19,000, reserves fall below $135 billion, or the BI-Rate rises above 5.75%.
- A bill to amend the 3% ceiling, or a 2026 deficit outturn above 2.85% of GDP.
- Any rise in subsidised fuel prices, and protest deaths or troops on Jakarta's streets.
- Whether the wiretapping case reaches beyond the police.
- Whether the election bill revives the selection of regional heads by local councils.
- The next Papua displacement count, and hotspot counts in Sumatra and Kalimantan from July 2027.

## Why my rating differs from the model
The model scores Indonesia at 36 (Moderate), 142nd of 203 countries, unchanged on last year, with full data coverage and one red flag for active armed conflict. The app's history starts at 40 (Elevated) in 2015; the score has been Moderate every year since, with a low of 33 in 2022 and 2023. I rate the country one tier higher.

The economic pillar (36) uses IMF figures for 2026 that describe a sound economy: growth of 5.1%, inflation of 3.3%, government debt of 41.7% of GDP and a deficit of 2.9%. They miss the price of risk: a 41.5% fall in equities, a record low for the rupiah, 100 basis points of rate rises in a month and a possible MSCI reclassification. Reserve cover (5.51 months of imports in 2025) is close to the 5.3 months Bank Indonesia reported for September.

The stability pillar (29) rests on annual data. Political stability was about 53 on the app's scale in 2025, almost unchanged from 52 in 2022, and the battle-deaths figure (0.02 per 100,000) is for 2024. The displacement indicator (0.01% of the population in 2025) counts only people who left the country, so it misses more than 120,000 Papuans displaced at home. In governance (51), control of corruption (about 38 in 2025) predates the police scandal. The model has no measure of fire or haze, and the app's watchlist holds no Indonesia events.

## Implications
Portfolio investors should plan around MSCI's November review and its June 2027 decision, with Hashim's 20,000 to 21,000 per dollar as a stress case. Since 1 June natural-resource exporters have had to hold dollar earnings in state banks, and the government plans to push them to convert more into rupiah. The environment ministry is verifying 355 companies whose land burned. Contracts tied to Singapore carry risk from the gas cut and the hold on power exports, and free meals suppliers should expect further cuts. Organisations in Papua's highlands should plan for military operations and attacks on civilians.
