## Bottom line up front
The Philippines' political risk is Elevated and rising. The feud between President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. and the Duterte family is now fought in the Senate, the courts and the police: Vice President Sara Duterte faces a Senate verdict on unexplained wealth, her father awaits trial at the ICC, a senator wanted by the same court has been in hiding since May, and Marcos's cousin, former Speaker Martin Romualdez, is in jail on plunder charges. Meanwhile growth slowed to 2.3% in the second quarter, inflation hit 7.2% in September and the peso passed 64 to the dollar. I would return the outlook to Stable if the verdict passes without sustained unrest and inflation falls below 5% by mid-2027.

## Key judgments
1. There is a **roughly even chance** (~50%) that Sara Duterte is still vice president on 1 November 2027. Conviction needs 14 votes: on 23 September the impeachment court decided to count only the 20 senators able to take part, not all 24, and on 30 September the Supreme Court dismissed petitions against that reading. The majority that elected Senate President Sherwin Gatchalian in June had 13, and the Dutertes keep allies such as Alan Peter Cayetano and Imee Marcos. I hold this with low confidence: one senator changing sides in June was enough to change the Senate leadership.
2. Rodrigo Duterte's ICC trial is **likely** (~60%) to open before 1 November 2027. Judges confirmed all charges on 23 April, and the trial is expected six to 18 months after confirmation. The main risk of delay is his defence's claim that, at 81, he is too frail to follow proceedings.
3. Dela Rosa is **unlikely** (~30%) to be in ICC custody by 1 November 2027. He left the Senate building in a colleague's car on 14 May and has not been seen since, and a one-week ultimatum with a P50 million bounty, announced on 23 October, had produced no arrest by 3 November.
4. Real GDP growth for 2026 is **likely** (~75%) to come in below 3.5%, the bottom of the government's reduced target. First-half growth was 2.6%, so the second half needs 4.4%, and infrastructure funds only began to flow at the end of June.
5. Headline inflation is **likely** (~70%) to be above 4% in the September 2027 reading. The central bank raised its 2027 forecast to 6.0% from 5.4%, citing oil and El Niño, and expects a return to target only in 2028. A settlement of the US-Israeli war on Iran that lowered oil prices is the most plausible way this fails.
6. A confrontation between Chinese and Philippine government vessels is **very unlikely** (~10%) to kill a Philippine crew member before 1 November 2027. China Coast Guard presence at Scarborough Shoal is the highest AMTI has recorded since it began regular tracking in 2019, and Chinese ships used water cannon in July, but Beijing has kept to water cannon and patrols, and the escorted Philippine fuel mission of 27 October passed without interference.

## Drivers
### Political
Marcos is limited to one six-year term, and the next presidential election is due on 8 May 2028. In May Reuters called Sara Duterte the clear favourite to succeed him; a conviction would remove her and bar her from elected office for life. The House impeached her for the second time on 11 May, by 257 votes to 25, after the Supreme Court had struck down the first impeachment in 2025 on procedural grounds.

The Senate then changed its president twice. On 11 May it elected Cayetano, a former foreign secretary under Rodrigo Duterte, by 13 votes to 9. On 3 June twelve senators declared all posts vacant, and on 17 June Joel Villanueva left Cayetano's bloc and gave Gatchalian the 13 votes he needed. On 13 October the impeachment court, with Francis Escudero presiding, let the prosecution drop three of its four articles, including the alleged assassination plot against the president. Only Article II remains: unexplained wealth of at least P817 million for her and her husband. The defence opens its case on 9 November, and lead prosecutor Gerville Luistro hopes the trial ends before Christmas.

The feud has also split the Marcos family. Senator Imee Marcos has allied herself with the Dutertes, and she and the president visited their father's grave separately before All Saints' Day.

The flood-control scandal reached the president's family on 7 September, when the Ombudsman charged Romualdez, Marcos's first cousin and Speaker until September 2025, with plunder over at least P7.44 billion in kickbacks from projects funded in 2022 to 2025. He is held at the New Quezon City Jail and his next bail hearing is on 12 November. At the October bail hearings a DPWH engineer said Romualdez was not among the proponents of the Bulacan flood-control projects, and a former DPWH undersecretary called by the prosecution said he had no direct dealings with him on kickbacks. Co-accused Zaldy Co is abroad and treated as a fugitive, and Ombudsman Jesus Crispin Remulla has promised more cases, including for money laundering.

### Security
China's presence at Scarborough Shoal (Bajo de Masinloc), which both countries claim, has grown sharply. AMTI counted 933 China Coast Guard ship-days there in the first half of 2026, against 1,099 in all of 2025 and 516 in 2024. Chinese ships fired water cannon at a Philippine fuel mission for fishermen on 23 and 24 July. In late October the coast guard said it was investigating unidentified equipment on a Chinese vessel inside the lagoon, first monitored on 3 October, and on 30 October China's Southern Theater Command reported "combat readiness patrols" around the shoal over unspecified Philippine provocations. Manila holds that the 2016 arbitral award is final and binding; Beijing rejects it.

The drug war cases keep the Duterte camp mobilised. Rodrigo Duterte is detained by the ICC in The Hague. The warrant against dela Rosa, made public on 11 May, covers at least 32 killings between 2016 and 2018, and Duterte supporters rallied in Pasay against it. On 3 November the Palace said police cannot choose whether to serve a valid warrant on their former chief.

In Mindanao, the first elected Bangsamoro Parliament held its inaugural session on 30 October. Its 80 members chose Abdulraof Macacua as chief minister over Moro Islamic Liberation Front chair Murad Ebrahim.

### Economic
Growth slowed to 2.3% in the second quarter, the weakest since 2009 outside the pandemic years. Household consumption grew only 2.8% and government construction fell 32.4%, with flood-control spending halted through 2026. Inflation reached a three-year high of 7.2% in September as oil prices rose with the war on Iran. The central bank raised its policy rate by 50 basis points to 5.5% on 22 October, its fourth straight increase, and left the door open to another in December. The peso has fallen nearly 10% since the war began and passed 64 to the dollar on 27 October; the central bank says it does not raise rates to defend it. For 2026 the model adds government debt of 60.5% of GDP, a fiscal deficit of 4.2% and a current-account deficit of 4.8%.

### Social
The government blamed the consumption slowdown on inflation, job losses and lower remittances caused by the Middle East conflict. Opposition lawmaker Chel Diokno has pressed Marcos for concrete measures to protect households from the weaker peso. The flood-control revelations drew large street protests that continued into November 2025.

## Indicators to watch
- The Senate vote on Article II, its tally, and any petition to the Supreme Court against the result.
- Rally turnout after the verdict or when the ICC trial opens.
- Dela Rosa's arrest or surrender, or evidence that police are protecting him.
- The Sandiganbayan ruling on Romualdez's bail, and new Ombudsman cases against sitting senators.
- Monthly inflation above 8%, the peso past 66 to the dollar, or another rate rise in December.
- China Coast Guard ship-days at Scarborough in AMTI's next count, and any fixed structure in the lagoon.
- Whether the ASEAN summit in mid-November produces a South China Sea code of conduct.

## Implications
Importers and firms with dollar costs face a peso past 64 and a central bank that says it is not raising rates to defend it. Public works contractors should expect slow fund releases and close audit while the Ombudsman files more cases. Fishing, survey and shipping operators near Scarborough Shoal face growing Chinese patrols. Gatherings in Metro Manila around the verdict and the mid-November ASEAN summit need security planning. Governments should expect the verdict and the 2028 succession to absorb Marcos's attention, and should plan for a Duterte-aligned candidate to be competitive in May 2028.
